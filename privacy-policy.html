Analytics

This site uses GoatCounter, a privacy-focused analytics tool, to understand basic site traffic — how many people visit, which pages they land on, and roughly where they're coming from. This helps us keep the resource menu useful and improve it over time.

What we don't do

GoatCounter does not use cookies and does not track individuals. It collects only aggregated, anonymous data: page views, referrers, browser type, approximate location (country-level), and screen size. No personally identifiable information is collected or stored, and visitors cannot be individually identified or followed across visits or other sites.

Legal basis

Where GDPR applies, the legal basis for this limited data collection is legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)) — understanding, in aggregate, how this resource is used so we can maintain and improve it.

Learn more

For further detail on what GoatCounter collects and how, see GoatCounter's own privacy policy.
